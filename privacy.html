Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Starcarriers Logistics LLC, the company that makes CarDeck ("CarDeck," "we," "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the CarDeck mobile app, web app and the website cardeckapp.com (together, the "Service").

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: company name, owner and dispatcher names, email addresses, phone numbers, and the company logo you upload.
  • Driver information: driver names and phone numbers, added by the company that the driver works with. We use phone numbers to text login codes.
  • Load and vehicle information: VINs, vehicle details, pickup and delivery addresses, photos, videos, inspection and damage notes, bills of lading, invoices and documents you upload.
  • Signatures: the customer's signature and name at pickup and delivery, whether they sign on the driver's phone or on their own phone through a one-time signing link, and the driver's saved signature. Signing links expire and can be used only once.
  • Broker and customer details: names, MC numbers, phone numbers, emails and billing addresses you save so invoices and loads fill in automatically.
  • Emails you forward to the Service: broker emails and attachments you send to CarDeck so loads can be created and updated automatically.
  • Location information: GPS location when you take photos, save a pickup or delivery spot, or share an ETA, only while you use those features and with your device's permission.
  • Payment information: subscription billing is handled by Apple, Google or our payment processor, depending on how you subscribe. We do not store full card numbers. Payment QR codes you upload are shown only to your company's users and customers.
  • Usage and device information: app version, device type, crash reports and basic usage data that help us keep the Service working.
  • Information kept on your phone: so the app works without signal, loads, changes and photos are saved on your device until they can be sent. Your truck size, maps app and similar preferences are also kept on your device.

2. How we use information

  • To provide the Service: create and manage loads, store records, and generate invoices and reports.
  • To read forwarded emails and documents with automated tools (including AI) so they become loads, delivery updates and records in your account.
  • To send account emails and text messages, such as login codes, monthly reports, reminders and service notices. Message and data rates may apply.
  • To send messages you ask us to send, such as ETA texts and signing links to your customers.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent fraud and fix problems.
  • To improve the Service.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

3. How information is shared

  • Within your company: owners and dispatchers can see their company's loads and records. Drivers see only the loads assigned to them, and the pay only if their company turns that on. When a company removes a driver, it can leave the driver read-only access to their past loads for up to 30 days.
  • When you choose to share: for example, ETA texts and signing links sent to customers, packets sent to your factoring company, or transfers to another carrier you choose.
  • Location notes: notes you mark "public" at business addresses can be seen by other CarDeck users and are shown without your name. Notes at home addresses are always private to your company.
  • Service providers: hosting, storage, email, text messaging, AI processing and payment providers that help us run the Service, only for that purpose.
  • Legal reasons: when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

4. How long we keep information

We keep load records, photos, signatures and documents for at least two years so they are available for damage claims and disputes, and longer while your account is active. When an account is closed, we delete or anonymize its information within a reasonable time, unless we must keep it for legal reasons.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including encrypted connections, to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you as required by law if a breach affects you.

6. Your choices and rights

  • You can view and update your account information in the app.
  • You can turn off location access in your device settings. Some features may not work without it.
  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by emailing roy@cardeckapp.com. You can also delete your account from the app.
  • Depending on your state (for example, California), you may have additional privacy rights. Contact us to use them.

7. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make important changes, we will let you know in the app or by email.

Everything a carrier needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Most trucking apps are built for someone in an office. CarDeck is built around how drivers really work.

AI reads your loads

Forward a broker email or a screenshot from Central Dispatch and the load fills itself in. You check it before you accept it.

Fast VIN scanning

Scan a VIN to add a car. Search any VIN later and see everything about it: photos, BOL, signatures and invoice.

Inspections that never stop you

The camera walks you around the car, upright or sideways. Mark damages on a realistic car drawing right from the camera. Every photo gets the date and place, and keeps shooting while photos upload.

Works offline

No signal at the auction? Keep working, even create new loads. Everything is saved on the phone and sends by itself over mobile data as soon as you're back online.

Deck view

See your truck, from a single-car to a 10-car hauler, top and bottom deck. Tap a spot to place each car, color-coded by where it's going.

Location notes

Where to park, who to call, how to find the gate. Drivers share tips at business addresses with a photo, vote them up or down, or keep private notes for their own company. Notes on brokers too.

One-tap ETA text

Send the customer a professional English message with the ETA, the car and the driver's name. No typing needed, whatever language the driver speaks.

Get paid on the spot

Customers scan your Zelle, Cash App or Venmo QR code and pay your company directly. On COD loads the driver sees exactly what to collect. CarDeck takes no cut.

Invoices and factoring

Bill one load or several from the same broker, add extra charges, or invoice a private customer. See what's owed, overdue and paid, send reminders, and send a complete packet to your factoring company in one tap.

Signatures your way

The customer signs on the driver's phone — turn it sideways for a bigger box — or touch-free from a text link on their own phone. "Customer not available" and STI stamps when nobody is there. The driver signs once and it's added every time.

BOL from the truck

A clean bill of lading with both signatures, ready to email or print right there — AirPrint, Wi-Fi or the portable Bluetooth printer in your truck.

Brokers remembered

Type two letters or the MC number and the broker's email, billing address, payment terms and inspection app fill in by themselves. Pickup and delivery places too.

Monthly report

On the 1st of every month you get the cars you moved, the money you earned, and what's paid and still owed. Also as a PDF for your accountant.

Records you can count on

Photos, BOLs, signatures and the original broker emails are kept for at least two years, ready for any damage claim.

Never miss a renewal

Keep insurance, DOT papers and other documents in the app, with reminders before they expire.

From one truck to a full fleet.

Turn on only what you need. The app stays simple for every driver.

Owner-operators

Ditch the paper. Run your loads, photos, invoices and payments from your phone.

Small carriers

Add drivers by phone number — they get a text to join. Assign loads from your phone, choose who sees the pay, and set how many photos each broker needs.

Growing fleets

Dispatchers get their own logins and move loads between drivers fast. Drivers who work with several companies switch between them in one app.

How it works

From broker email to paid. No double work.

01

Load builds itself

AI reads the broker email or a Central Dispatch screenshot: VINs, pickup, delivery, pay and terms.

02

Load and inspect

The camera walks you around each car. Mark damage and place every car on the deck.

03

Text the ETA, get signed

One tap sends your ETA in professional English. Customers confirm or sign from a text link.

Get paid

Show your Zelle, Cash App or Venmo QR, or send the invoice or factoring packet in one tap.

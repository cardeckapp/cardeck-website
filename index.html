How it works

From broker email to paid. No double work.

Some brokers make you use their app too. Fine. CarDeck reads their emails and keeps the record, so nobody does the same job twice.

01

Load builds itself

AI reads the broker email or a Central Dispatch screenshot: VINs, pickup, delivery, pay and terms.

02

Load and inspect

The camera walks you around each car. Mark damage and place every car on the deck.

03

One tap sends your ETA in professional English. Customers confirm or sign from a text link.

Show your Zelle, Cash App or Venmo QR, or send the invoice or factoring packet in one tap.

Questions

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Photos, inspections, signatures and even new loads save on the phone and send by themselves when the signal comes back.

Can my drivers use Spanish, Russian or Ukrainian?

Yes. Each driver picks their language. Papers and texts that go to the customer are always in English.

Do I still need the broker apps?

Sometimes, when a broker requires it. CarDeck reads the broker's emails, keeps the full record and tells the driver when a broker requires its own app.

Why is the price different in the App Store?

The prices on this page are for paying on cardeckapp.com. You can also subscribe inside the app with Apple or Google: $23.99 founding, $34.99 standard and $11.99 per extra driver, because the app price includes the store's fee.

When does CarDeck launch?

On the App Store and Google Play in 2027. Sign up now to save your founding spot.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts. Cancel from the app or the website. Your founding price stays as long as your account is active.

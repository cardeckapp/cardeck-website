How it works

Pickup to paid in three steps

Step 1

Load & inspect

The camera walks you around each car. Mark damage, then place every car on the deck.

Step 2

Text the ETA, get signed

One tap sends the customer your ETA. They sign on your phone or from a text link.

Step 3

Get paid

Show your Zelle, Cash App or Venmo QR, or send the invoice before you leave.

Two apps down to one

Questions

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Photos, inspections, signatures and even new loads are saved on the phone and send by themselves when you're back online.

Can my drivers use it in Spanish, Russian or Ukrainian?

Yes. Each driver picks their own language. Papers and texts that go to customers stay in English.

I still have to use a broker's app for some loads. Does CarDeck help?

Yes. CarDeck reads the broker's emails and keeps the full record, and tells the driver when a broker requires its own app.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts. Cancel from the app or the website. Your founding price stays as long as your account stays active.

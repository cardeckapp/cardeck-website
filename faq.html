FAQ

Questions

Can prices change?
Standard prices

They may change in the future. If you're already a customer, we'll always email you at least 30 days before any price change.

Founding prices

Locked for life as long as your account stays active. If you cancel, the founding price is gone and can't be restored.

Website vs. in-app

Price changes apply to subscriptions bought on cardeckapp.com. Subscriptions bought inside the app follow Apple's and Google's rules for price changes. Your founding price stays locked either way.

How does the founding offer work?
Who gets it

The first 100 companies, or anyone who signs up in the first 90 days after the app launches, whichever comes first.

What it costs

$19.99/month, plus $9.99/month for each of up to 3 extra drivers. Any drivers beyond those 3 are $14.99/month each.

How long it lasts

For life, as long as your account stays active. If you cancel, you lose the founding price for good.

Is it cheaper on the website?

Yes. Plans cost less when you subscribe on cardeckapp.com. Inside the app, Apple and Google add their fee to the price.

Does it work without signal?

Yes. Photos, inspections, signatures and even new loads save on the phone and send by themselves when the signal comes back.

Can my drivers use Spanish, Russian or Ukrainian?

Yes. Each driver picks their language. Papers and texts that go to the customer are always in English.

Do I still need the broker apps?

Sometimes, when a broker requires it. CarDeck reads the broker's emails, keeps the full record and tells the driver when a broker requires its own app.

When does CarDeck launch?

On the App Store and Google Play in 2027. Sign up now to save your founding spot.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contracts. Cancel from the app or the website. If you're a founding member, canceling ends your founding price for good.
